ABOUT KENYA MABATI

Your Building-Materials Store.

Kenya Mabati is a building-materials supplier based in Ruiru along Kamiti Road, serving customers across Kenya.

Our catalogue covers roofing (Box Profile, Corrugated, Dumuzaz, Roman Tile, Versatile, Eurotile, Orientile and Zee Tile mabati), steel bars, round bars, binding wire, roofing nails, Simba cement, barbed wire, chainlink, kuku net, timber and Kentank water tanks.

We focus on clear product options, competitive pricing and convenient ordering. Whether you know exactly what you need or you are still planning, our team is available to help.
